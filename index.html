Got questions

QUICK ANSWERS

How long does delivery take?

Printing takes 2-3 days and delivery 2-4 days, so about 4-6 working days in total.

How do I pay?

We share payment details (UPI) on WhatsApp after your design and size are confirmed. Cash on delivery can be arranged on request.

Can I print my own design or lines?

Yes. Send your own quote, name, photo or artwork on WhatsApp. Please only send designs you own or have the right to print.

What mugs do you print on?

11 oz ceramic mugs in white, black, navy or maroon. You can print on one side or both sides. Send your name, quote or logo on WhatsApp.

Which size should I choose?

Oversized tees run roomy, so take your usual size for a loose fit or one size down for a regular fit. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will guide you.

Can I return a printed T-shirt?

Custom tees are made just for you, so we cannot take returns for size or design choice. If there is a print or fabric defect, message us and we will make it right.
